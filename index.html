01 · Our Story

Moroccan warmth,
made contemporary.

Kenz means “treasure”. Ours is simple: exceptional coffee, thoughtful details, and a space that feels familiar from the first visit.

We pair specialty beans with subtle Moroccan references—natural plaster, walnut, brushed brass and handcrafted ceramics—without losing the calm simplicity of a modern café.

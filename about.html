Our Story

A little treasure
in Fès.

Kenz is a fictional café concept created to explore how a warm Moroccan identity can live inside a restrained, contemporary digital experience.

01 · The idea

Coffee culture with a distinctly Moroccan sense of welcome.

Kenz means “treasure”. The idea began with a simple question: what would a specialty coffee house in Fès look like if it felt modern without becoming anonymous?

The answer is a mix of quiet materials, thoughtful typography, excellent coffee and small references to Moroccan craft. Nothing is loud. Every detail is meant to make the space feel calm, tactile and familiar.

02 · What matters
01

Quality

Seasonal beans, careful extraction and a focused menu rather than endless choice.

02

Warmth

Hospitality comes before performance. The space should feel easy to return to.

03

Craft

Handmade ceramics, honest materials and subtle Moroccan references shape the experience.
